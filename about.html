Our Story

Built on a Belief in Clean Energy

Since 2010, we've been on a mission to make solar power accessible, affordable and reliable for every Australian.

Our Mission

Powering a Sustainable Future — One Roof at a Time

SolarVision Energy was founded in Sydney in 2010 by a group of engineers and environmentalists who shared a simple belief: everyone deserves access to clean, affordable energy.

Over the past 14 years we've grown from a small rooftop installer into one of Australia's most trusted solar companies, with teams operating in every major state. But our values have never changed — we put customers first, we use only the best equipment, and we back every installation with industry-leading warranties and support.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2010

Founded in Sydney

SolarVision started with a team of 5 in a Sydney garage, installing our first residential systems on the Northern Beaches.

2013

500th Installation

We hit our 500th installation milestone and expanded operations to Melbourne and Brisbane.

2016

Commercial Division Launched

We launched our dedicated commercial solar team, completing our first 1MW+ project for a large industrial client.

2019

Battery Storage Introduced

We partnered with leading battery manufacturers to bring home energy storage solutions to our residential customers.

2022

National Expansion

SolarVision now operates in all 6 Australian states and 2 territories with over 200 full-time staff.

2024

5,000+ Installations & Award Win

We surpassed 5,000 installations and were named "Best Solar Installer" at the Australian Clean Energy Awards.
